How to calculate a tip

Multiply the bill amount by the tip percentage to find the tip. Then add the tip to the bill.

Bill × (Tip % ÷ 100) = Tip

For example, a $100 bill with a 15% tip means:

$100 × 0.15 = $15 tip

$100 + $15 = $115 total

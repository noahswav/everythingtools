How to calculate a discount

First, calculate the discount amount by multiplying the original price by the discount percentage. Then subtract the discount from the original price.

Original price × (Discount ÷ 100)

For example, 20% off $100 means:

$100 × 0.20 = $20 discount

$100 − $20 = $80 sale price
